Projects

Research prototypes

Layika — Tabla Glove

ESP32 · FreeRTOS · ESP-NOW · MIDI

Swarings

MIDI controller · touch & rotation sensing

Rings on a pipe: touch plays a note, sliding bends pitch, rotating modulates. Patented; UIST 2025; played live in NIME concerts.

GraviTone

OpenCV · Max/MSP · TouchDesigner · OSC/MIDI

Balls orbit a gravity well on stretch fabric; real-time camera tracking of speed and position drives synths and visuals. Built the ball-tracking prototype. NIME 2025.

PathBuddy
